	Title: 5 Things to Consider when Building Machine Learning Projects

	Summary: This article highlights five considerations for building machine learning projects: 1) Define a specific problem aligned with business goals to ensure the project's relevance and impact. 2) Prepare and preprocess data meticulously, as the quality of input data directly affects model performance. 3) Embrace iteration, recognizing that initial models may not be accurate and that continuous refinement is necessary. 4) Monitor models in production to detect and address issues promptly, ensuring sustained performance. 5) Ensure scalability of the machine learning pipeline to accommodate growing data volumes and evolving project demands.

	Title: Complexity of ML Projects: Challenges and Best Practices
	URL: https://www.analyticsinsight.net/machine-learning/complexity-of-ml-projects-challenges-and-best-practices
	Summary: This article explores factors contributing to the complexity of machine learning projects, such as data characteristics (volume, variety, quality), model type, project requirements (accuracy, interpretability), and deployment considerations (scalability, security). It discusses challenges like extended development times, high computational demands, and maintenance difficulties. Best practices suggested include defining clear objectives, adopting a data-centric approach, iterative development, modular design, version control, performance monitoring, utilizing cloud-based infrastructure, and assembling a diverse team with expertise in data science, software engineering, domain knowledge, and project management.

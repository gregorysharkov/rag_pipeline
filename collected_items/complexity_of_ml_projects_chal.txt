	Title: Complexity of ML Projects: Challenges and Best Practices
	URL: https://www.analyticsinsight.net/machine-learning/complexity-of-ml-projects-challenges-and-best-practices
	Summary: This article explores factors contributing to the complexity of machine learning projects, such as data volume, variety, and quality, as well as model type and project requirements. It discusses challenges like extended development times, high computational costs, and difficulties in interpretability. Best practices suggested include defining clear objectives, adopting a data-centric approach, iterative development, modular design, version control, performance monitoring, utilizing cloud-based infrastructure, and assembling a diverse team with skills in data science, software engineering, domain knowledge, and project management.

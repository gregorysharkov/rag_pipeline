	Summary: This article explores factors contributing to the complexity of machine learning projects, such as data characteristics (volume, variety, quality), model types, and project requirements like accuracy and interpretability. It discusses challenges like extended development times, high computational costs, and maintenance issues. Best practices include defining clear objectives, adopting a data-centric approach, iterative development, modular design, version control, performance monitoring, utilizing cloud-based infrastructure, and assembling a diverse team with expertise in data science, software engineering, domain knowledge, and project management.
